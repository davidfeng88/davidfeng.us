## Checkmate

In formal games, it is usually considered good etiquette to resign an inevitably lost game before being checkmated.

### Forcing moves (先手)

1. Check move
2. Threat
3. Attack, take

有顺序。

### Puzzles - checkmate

1. Find all the check moves, see if we have proper follow-ups.
2. 分数少要抓紧时间进攻。（Eddie 9:41回答问题，Q蛋）
3. Damiano's Mate.
4. check moves没有好的后续，可以开始找threat。

## opening principles

1. control the center
2. Develop minor pieces 
3. Castle your king

### 苏格兰开局

1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. d4

### 意大利开局

1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4 Bc5

白方可以：

1. castle
2. Nc3
3. d3
4. (book move) c3，后续 4...Nf6, 5.d4 exd4 6.cxd4 Bb4+ 7. Bd2 Bxd2+ 8. Nbxd2 d5 9. exd5 Nxd5 10. Qb3 (double attack N at d5 & b2 if black moves the Bishop) Nce7 11. 0-0 0-0 12. Rfe1 c6
